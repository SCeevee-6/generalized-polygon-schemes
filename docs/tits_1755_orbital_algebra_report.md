# Orbital algebra of the Tits group on 1755 points

## 1. Setting
- $G=T={}^2F_4(2)'$ has order 17,971,200. $H=2.[2^8]{:}5{:}4$ has order 10,240. $\Omega=G/H$ and $|\Omega|=1755$.
- $\Omega$ is the point set of the Ree–Tits generalized octagon GO(2,4), of order $(s,t)=(2,4)$. The action is primitive of rank 5 with subdegrees 1, 10, 80, 640, 1024.
- The valency-10 orbital $R_1$ is the collinearity graph of GO(2,4). It is distance-regular with intersection array $\{10,8,8,8;1,1,1,5\}$ and automorphism group $T{:}2$ ("On some distance-regular graphs with many vertices", arXiv:1809.10197, Table 13).
- That array agrees with the octagon formulas $b_0=s(t+1)$, $b_{1,2,3}=st$, $c_{1,2,3}=1$ and $c_4=t+1$. The point count agrees too: $(s+1)(st+1)(s^2t^2+1)=1755$.
- $R_i$ is the set of pairs at distance $i$ in $R_1$. This was verified on the actual permutation action (Section 6).

The Bose–Mesner algebra $\langle A_0,\dots,A_4\rangle$ is 5-dimensional, commutative and symmetric. It is generated by $A_1$:
- $A_2=A_1^2-A_1-10I$
- $A_3=A_1A_2-A_2-8A_1$
- $A_4=(A_1A_3-A_3-8A_2)/5$

So the scheme is P-polynomial (metric).

- **Permutation character.** The multiplicities 1, 351, 650, 675, 78 give a multiplicity-free decomposition. These values are irreducible degrees of $T$ according to the ATLAS character table, which was not recomputed here.
- **Krein parameters.** All $q^k_{ij}\ge0$, so the Krein conditions hold. The only vanishing nontrivial parameter is $q^4_{44}=0$, on the 78-dimensional eigenspace with $\theta=-5$.
- **Not cometric.** None of the 24 orderings of $E_1,\dots,E_4$ is Q-polynomial. The scheme is metric but not cometric, so it is not formally self-dual.

## 4. Fusions
Merging classes into blocks $\{0\},B_1,\dots,B_t$ gives an association scheme iff the sum $\sum_{i\in B_m,j\in B_n}p^k_{ij}$ is constant over $k$ in each block. All 15 partitions of $\{1,2,3,4\}$ were tested ((project artifact)):

**Conclusion.** Only the two trivial fusions close: the original 4-class scheme and the 1-class scheme of $K_{1755}$. There is no nontrivial fusion, hence no strongly regular graph, and no union of orbitals forms a scheme of intermediate rank.

**Obstructions.**
- $\{1\}\mid\{2,3,4\}$ would make $R_1$ strongly regular. That fails because the common-neighbour counts of non-adjacent pairs are not constant: $p^2_{11}=1$ but $p^3_{11}=p^4_{11}=0$.
- For "distance $\le 2$" ($\{1,2\}\mid\{3,4\}$), the counts $\sum_{i,j\in\{1,2\}}p^h_{ij}$ are 25 for $h=1$ but 9 for $h=2$, and 3 for $h=3$ but 5 for $h=4$.

The strongly regular graphs $T$ does afford, $(1600,351,94,72)$ and $(3510,693,180,126)$, come from other actions (arXiv:1809.10197). They are not fusions of this scheme.

## 5. Geometric reading
- $R_1$ is collinearity in GO(2,4). Since $a_1=1$, each point lies on $t+1=5$ lines of size 3, giving $1755\cdot5/3=2925$ lines. Those lines are the points of the dual octagon GO(4,2), the 2925-vertex DRG of arXiv:1809.10197.
- $R_4$ is opposition, with $k_4=s^4t^3=1024$. Its eigenvalues on $E_1,\dots,E_4$ are $-16,16,-16,64$.

## 6. Verification on the explicit permutation representation
The ATLAS v3 standard generators of $T$ on 1755 points (TF42G1-p1755B0) were downloaded in GAP format.
- The orbit of point 1 has size 1755.
- The stabilizer orbits, computed from Schreier generators, have sizes 1, 10, 80, 640, 1024.
- Counting $p^k_{ij}$ directly from the action reproduces all 125 values.
- $R_1$ is symmetric and 10-regular, and BFS distance equals the orbital label at all 1755 points.
