## Found

- De Bruyn and Vanhove, On Q-polynomial regular near 2d-gons, Combinatorica (2015), doi
  10.1007/s00493-014-3039-x. Covers the Q-polynomial question for thick octagons.
- De Bruyn, A combinatorial characterization of extremal generalized hexagons, doi 10.37236/9245; and
  Extremal generalized hexagons and triple intersection numbers, Discrete Math. (2021), doi
  10.1016/j.disc.2020.112280.
- Breuer and Lux, Comm. Algebra 24 (1996), doi 10.1080/00927879608825701. The database shipped with ctbllib
  1.3.11 has 26 sporadic groups and 12 automorphism groups and does not include the Tits group
  (`tits1755/gap/extra_checks_output.txt`).
- Crnković, Rukavina and Švob, J. Algebraic Combin. 51 (2020), doi 10.1007/s10801-019-00888-5: the
  1755-point graph, and the SRG(1600, 351, 94, 72) with automorphism group T (Theorem 8, Table 12).
- arXiv:2510.25410 (Strongly regular graphs of rank four): lists the same SRG(1600, 351, 94, 72) for 2F4(2)'
  with stabiliser L3(3).2 as an exceptional rank-four example.
- General fusion literature, none of it on generalized polygons: Kharaghani et al., Non-commutative association
  schemes and their fusion association schemes (FFA 2018, doi 10.1016/j.ffa.2018.03.007); van Dam and
  coauthors on amorphic schemes and fusions of pairs (JCTA 2025, doi 10.1016/j.jcta.2025.106045); Herman et al.
  on isolating fusions (arXiv 2205.01810); Sankey, Weighted association schemes, fusions, and minimal coherent
  closures (doi 10.1007/s10801-014-0553-2).
- Ree–Tits octagon geometry: Coolsaet (embeddings), Van Maldeghem (Proc. LMS 1998, doi
  10.1112/s0024611598000070), Belousov (automorphisms of GO(2,4)), Ihringer (partial ovoids).

## Not found

- Any paper on the coherent configurations of T on cosets of A6.2^2 or 5^2:4A4.
- Any use of the Bannai–Muzychuk criterion on polygon schemes.
